Engage Your Leads Instantly

Connect your Odoo CRM with Nyife WhatsApp Business API to automatically send personalized WhatsApp template messages whenever key events happen on your leads and opportunities.

Whether a new lead is created, a stage changes, or a deal is won, your customers get instant WhatsApp notifications with dynamic content pulled directly from Odoo fields.

Supported CRM Events

>

Notify customers when their deal progresses. Filter by specific stages.

Send congratulations or next steps when a deal is successfully won.
